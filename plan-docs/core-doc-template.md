# 1. Establishment, purpose, and plan structure

${sponsor.legalName} (the **Employer** or **Plan Sponsor**) establishes ${plan.name} (the **Plan**) to provide the employee welfare benefits identified in adopted Benefit Appendices.

The Plan consists of this Core Plan Document, Appendix A, Appendix B, Appendix C, each adopted Benefit Appendix, and each schedule or amendment incorporated by those documents. Together, those materials state the governing terms of the Plan. The Plan is intended to be maintained as an employee welfare benefit plan under ERISA except to the extent an adopted Benefit Appendix identifies a benefit to which ERISA does not apply.

This Core Plan Document states rules shared across the Plan. A Benefit Appendix states the promise, eligibility rules, funding method, limitations, claims rules, and other terms specific to a benefit. A schedule may state operational or frequently changing details only within the authority granted by the document incorporating it.

# 2. Document hierarchy and interpretation

The Plan will be administered using the following order of authority:

1. Applicable law controls.
2. A Benefit Appendix controls over this Core Plan Document only for the benefit and subject the Benefit Appendix expressly addresses.
3. This Core Plan Document controls all remaining Plan-wide matters.
4. An incorporated schedule controls operational detail only within the authority granted by the governing document.
5. An insurance policy or service contract controls the obligations of the insurer or service provider, but does not amend the Employer's Plan unless the policy or contract is expressly incorporated for that purpose.
6. An SPD, SBC, enrollment page, provider directory, or administrative communication summarizes or implements the Plan and does not amend it unless adopted through the Plan's amendment procedure.

If two Benefit Appendices appear to govern the same benefit and subject, the later effective adopted document identified in Appendix B controls. The Plan Administrator will document the resolution rather than administer conflicting terms indefinitely.

Headings aid navigation and do not limit the operative text. A provision that is invalid or unenforceable will be applied to the maximum lawful extent without invalidating the remaining Plan.

# 3. Definitions

Unless a Benefit Appendix supplies a more specific definition for its benefit:

**Benefit Appendix** means a document adopted by the Employer that establishes or describes a specific benefit under the Plan.

**Code** means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

**Dependent** means a person eligible for a benefit under the applicable Benefit Appendix. Tax-dependent status is determined separately when relevant to the tax treatment of a benefit.

**Employee** means an individual classified by the Employer as its common-law employee and included in an eligible class under Appendix A or an applicable Benefit Appendix. A person's legal status, rather than a label alone, controls where applicable law requires it.

**Employer** means the Plan Sponsor and, for its own eligible Employees, each Participating Employer identified in Appendix A.

**ERISA** means the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, as amended.

**Participant** means an Employee or other person who has satisfied the applicable eligibility and enrollment rules and whose participation has not ended.

**Participating Employer** means a related or successor employer that has adopted the Plan and is identified in Appendix A.

**Plan Administrator** means the person or entity identified in Appendix A.

**Plan Year** means the period identified in Appendix A.

**Schedule** means a versioned operational document expressly incorporated by this Core Plan Document or a Benefit Appendix.

# 4. Participating employers

An employer other than the Plan Sponsor participates only after adopting the Plan through a written instrument or the adoption method identified in Appendix A. Each Participating Employer is responsible for providing accurate workforce and payroll information, making required contributions for its Employees, and performing the duties assigned to it by the Plan Administrator.

The Plan Sponsor may accept, condition, or terminate a Participating Employer's participation. Withdrawal of one Participating Employer does not terminate the Plan for the Plan Sponsor or another Participating Employer. Appendix A must identify the effective and, when applicable, termination date of each Participating Employer.

# 5. Eligibility, enrollment, and participation

The general eligible class and waiting period appear in Appendix A. A Benefit Appendix may establish different or additional requirements for that benefit. Eligibility for one benefit does not establish eligibility for another.

An eligible person becomes a Participant in a benefit by completing the required enrollment or election process and satisfying any required contribution, evidence, or coverage condition. Coverage begins on the date determined under the applicable Benefit Appendix and enrollment materials.

Participation ends when the earliest applicable event occurs: the person no longer satisfies eligibility requirements; employment or the required employment classification ends; required contributions are not timely paid after any required notice or grace period; the Participant makes a permitted election ending coverage; the benefit or Plan terminates; or another event specified in the applicable Benefit Appendix occurs.

Appendix A states the Plan's general rules for rehire, transfer between eligibility classes, and approved leave. A Benefit Appendix controls when the benefit or applicable law requires a different result. Statutory continuation and restoration rights, including rights applicable to qualifying family, medical, or uniformed-service leave, will be administered as required by law.

# 6. Elections and Section 125 treatment

When the Employer permits pre-tax payment or another qualified benefit election, the election is administered under the Employer's cafeteria plan identified in Appendix A. The cafeteria plan—not this general reference—states the binding-election rule, permitted midyear changes, election deadlines, salary-reduction terms, and correction authority.

An election affects only the benefits and coverage period to which it applies. Tax treatment depends on the Code, the terms of the applicable arrangement, and the facts applicable to the Participant. The Plan Administrator may make or require a prospective correction reasonably necessary to administer an election, satisfy nondiscrimination requirements, correct payroll treatment, or preserve the intended tax treatment, subject to applicable law.

# 7. Contributions and funding

A Benefit Appendix identifies whether the benefit is insured, paid from the Employer's general assets, funded through a trust or other separate arrangement, or funded through a combination of sources. It also identifies the applicable Employer and Participant contribution method.

Participant contributions may be collected through pre-tax salary reduction, after-tax payroll deduction, direct payment, or another lawful method. Employer contributions may be fixed, formula-based, conditional, matching, or discretionary as stated in the applicable Benefit Appendix or contribution schedule.

A bookkeeping account records rights and transactions but does not itself establish a trust or give a Participant an ownership interest in Employer assets. Plan assets, if any, will be identified and handled according to the documents and law governing those assets.

Refunds, dividends, credits, recoveries, and similar amounts will be allocated or applied according to the source of the payment, applicable contract terms, the Plan's funding method, and applicable law. They do not automatically belong exclusively to the Employer or to Participants.

# 8. Plan administration

The Employer appoints the Plan Administrator identified in Appendix A. The Plan Administrator has the authority necessary to administer the Plan consistently with its terms and applicable law, including authority to:

- interpret Plan provisions and resolve ambiguities;
- determine eligibility, participation, and benefit entitlement;
- establish reasonable enrollment, election, claim, appeal, documentation, and recordkeeping procedures;
- request information reasonably necessary to administer the Plan;
- delegate identified functions to insurers, claims administrators, service providers, committees, or professionals and monitor those delegations;
- decide administrative questions and classify requests according to their substance;
- correct eligibility, election, contribution, allocation, and payment errors;
- recover overpayments or provide underpayments using a reasonable method permitted by law;
- maintain records showing material decisions, money movement, and the authority for each action; and
- adopt administrative practices that apply uniformly to similarly situated persons.

A delegation does not transfer an obligation that the Plan or applicable law assigns to the Employer or Plan Administrator unless the governing document and law permit that transfer. Appendix A identifies material service providers and the functions assigned to them.

The Plan Administrator may rely reasonably on information supplied by a Participant, Employer, insurer, service provider, accountant, attorney, clinician, or other qualified source. Reliance does not authorize the Plan Administrator to disregard known contradictory information or avoid a required review.

# 9. Participant responsibilities and correction of errors

A Participant must provide complete and accurate information reasonably needed to determine eligibility, administer an election, substantiate a claim, coordinate benefits, or satisfy reporting requirements. A Participant must promptly report a material change affecting eligibility, coverage, or payment.

When an error occurs, the Plan Administrator will determine what should have occurred under the governing terms, document the correction, and take reasonable action to place the Plan and affected person in that position to the extent administratively practicable and legally permitted. Corrections may include adjusting records, contributions, payroll treatment, coverage, or payments; recovering an overpayment; or paying an amount previously underpaid.

Before materially adverse recovery action, the Plan Administrator will provide an explanation and any review opportunity required by the Plan or applicable law. A correction will not create coverage or tax treatment prohibited by law.

# 10. Claims, appeals, and payment

A person may request a benefit or coverage determination to which the person believes they are entitled. The applicable Benefit Appendix and incorporated claims procedure identify how and where to file, required information, decision timeframes, appeal rights, authorized decision-makers, and any external review rights.

Claims will be decided fairly and consistently under the governing Plan terms. An adverse benefit determination will state the material reason, identify the controlling term, describe information needed to perfect the claim when applicable, and explain the available review process.

The Plan may require reasonable proof of eligibility, expense, service, loss, or other facts material to a claim. If a person entitled to payment cannot receive or manage it, the Plan Administrator may direct payment to a legal representative, guardian, provider, or other appropriate recipient for that person's benefit when permitted by law. The Plan Administrator must document reasonable efforts to locate a missing payee before applying the Plan's unclaimed-payment procedure.

# 11. Assignment, coordination, and legal orders

A Benefit Appendix states whether and to what extent a Participant may assign a claim or direct payment to a provider. An assignment or payment direction does not grant rights beyond those provided by the Plan.

The Plan will honor a qualified medical child support order and another legally enforceable payment or coverage direction to the extent applicable. Benefit-specific coordination, subrogation, reimbursement, and recovery rights must be stated in the applicable Benefit Appendix or incorporated procedure.

# 12. Privacy and information governance

The Plan will use and disclose personal information only as permitted by the documents and law governing that information. Where HIPAA applies, the Plan Sponsor and Plan will maintain the required plan-document provisions, separation, safeguards, policies, notices, complaint process, and designated privacy and security responsibilities identified in the HIPAA Plan Administration Appendix.

Access to protected health information for Plan administration is limited to the persons and functions identified in that appendix or another authorized record. Employment decisions may not use protected health information received from the Plan except pursuant to a valid authorization or other lawful basis.

# 13. Participant disclosures and controlling records

The Plan Administrator will furnish SPDs, summaries of material modifications, claims notices, privacy notices, and other disclosures required for an applicable benefit. Participant-facing materials will be generated from or reconciled to the same adopted terms and versioned operational schedules used to administer the Plan.

An SPD explains the Plan but does not silently create, remove, or amend a benefit. When a participant communication reveals an inconsistency or omission, the Plan Administrator will investigate, correct the governing or participant-facing material as appropriate, preserve the affected versions, and provide any required notice.

# 14. Nondiscrimination

The Plan is intended to comply with each nondiscrimination rule applicable to an adopted benefit. The Plan Administrator may require reasonable election, contribution, or benefit adjustments permitted by law to correct or prevent a test failure. The applicable Benefit Appendix or cafeteria-plan document identifies the affected test and correction method.

# 15. Amendment and termination

The Employer may amend or terminate the Plan, this Core Plan Document, or a Benefit Appendix by the adoption method identified in Appendix A, subject to applicable law and any vested claim or benefit rights that may not lawfully be reduced.

An amendment must identify the affected document, version, effective date, and substantive change. The Plan Administrator will retain the prior version and determine which version governs based on the applicable event, service, claim, or coverage date.

Termination of one Benefit Appendix does not terminate another benefit or the Plan unless the termination instrument expressly says so. Claims arising from covered events before termination remain subject to the terms governing those events, except as lawfully amended.

# 16. No employment contract or tax guarantee

The Plan does not guarantee employment, continued employment, compensation, or any particular benefit beyond the governing terms. Employment remains subject to the Employer's employment policies and applicable law.

The Employer and Plan Administrator do not guarantee an individual's federal, state, or local tax result. The Plan will report and withhold amounts as the Plan Administrator reasonably determines applicable law requires, and will correct discovered reporting or withholding errors through the legally available process.

# 17. Governing law

The Plan is governed by applicable federal law, including ERISA and the Code where they apply. State law governs only to the extent it applies and is not preempted. A Benefit Appendix may identify additional law applicable to that benefit.

# 18. Incorporation and execution

Appendix A states the Plan's adopted identifying and administrative information. Appendix B identifies the Benefit Appendices currently adopted into the Plan. Appendix C incorporates the Enrollment Schedule that states the allowances or contributions applicable to eligible Participants without restating class-specific amounts in the Plan Document. A document not identified or otherwise validly adopted is not part of the Plan merely because it is distributed with Plan materials.

This Core Plan Document becomes effective on the effective date stated above and remains in effect until amended, replaced, or terminated according to the Plan.

By the adoption record identified in Appendix A, the Employer establishes this Core Plan Document and the Benefit Appendices identified in Appendix B.
