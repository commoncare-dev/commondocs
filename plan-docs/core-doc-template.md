# Welfare Benefit Plan
## Core Plan Document

Version: 1.0

---

# Establishment of the Plan

The Employer establishes this Welfare Benefit Plan ("Plan") to provide employee welfare benefits and related benefit programs for eligible employees and their eligible dependents.

This document establishes the legal framework of the Plan. The specific benefits offered under the Plan are described in one or more Benefit Appendices incorporated into this document by reference.

This Plan is intended to be maintained as an employee welfare benefit plan under the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 ("ERISA"), unless a particular Benefit Appendix states that ERISA does not apply to that benefit.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this Plan is to provide a flexible framework through which the Employer may offer one or more employee benefit programs.

Depending upon the Benefit Appendices adopted by the Employer, the Plan may include insured benefits, self-funded benefits, health reimbursement arrangements, health flexible spending arrangements, wellness benefits, voluntary benefits, or other employer-sponsored benefit programs permitted by law.

Each adopted Benefit Appendix forms part of this Plan.

---

# Governing Law

This Plan shall be interpreted under applicable federal law, including ERISA and the Internal Revenue Code, together with any other applicable federal laws governing employee benefit plans.

Where federal law permits, applicable state law shall govern matters not preempted by ERISA.

If any provision of this Plan conflicts with applicable law, only the conflicting provision shall be interpreted to comply with the law. The remainder of the Plan will continue in effect.

---

# Definitions

Unless a Benefit Appendix provides otherwise:

Benefit Appendix means any document adopted by the Employer that describes a specific benefit offered through this Plan.

Dependent means a person eligible for coverage under the rules of the applicable Benefit Appendix.

Employee means an individual classified by the Employer as eligible to participate under the applicable Benefit Appendix.

Employer means the employer identified in Appendix A.

Participant means an Employee or other eligible individual enrolled in one or more benefits under this Plan.

Plan means this Welfare Benefit Plan together with all incorporated appendices and amendments.

Plan Administrator means the person or entity identified in Appendix A.

Plan Year means the twelve-month period identified in Appendix A.

---

# Plan Structure

This Plan consists of this Core Plan Document together with one or more Benefit Appendices adopted by the Employer.

The Core Plan Document establishes the general rules governing the Plan. Each Benefit Appendix establishes the rules for a specific benefit.

Unless a Benefit Appendix states otherwise, the provisions of this Core Plan Document apply to every benefit offered through the Plan.

If two Benefit Appendices conflict, the Employer’s written adoption of the later appendix controls for that benefit only.

Each Benefit Appendix may describe, as applicable:

- eligibility requirements
- effective dates
- covered benefits
- reimbursement rules
- contribution requirements
- funding arrangements
- limitations
- termination provisions
- claims procedures, if applicable

If this Core Plan Document and a Benefit Appendix address the same subject, the Benefit Appendix controls only for that specific benefit. This Core Plan Document continues to govern all other matters.

---

# Section 125 Elections

The Employer permits Participants to pay for eligible benefits through a cafeteria plan intended to satisfy Section 125 of the Internal Revenue Code.

When permitted by applicable law, Employees may direct eligible compensation, salary reductions, employer-provided benefit allowances, flexible employer contributions, or other eligible amounts toward benefits available under this Plan.

Whether a particular benefit receives favorable tax treatment depends upon the Internal Revenue Code and the rules governing that benefit. Nothing in this Plan guarantees that every available benefit qualifies for pre-tax treatment.

The rules applicable to any Section 125 arrangement are described in the applicable Benefit Appendix.

---

# Eligibility

Eligibility for each benefit is superseded by the applicable Benefit Appendix. If not specified, the general plan eligiblity requirements are specified in Appendix A.

Participation in one benefit does not automatically make an individual eligible for another benefit.

The Employer reserves the right to establish different eligibility rules for different benefit programs where permitted by law.

---

# Funding

Benefits offered through this Plan may be funded entirely by the Employer, entirely by Participants, or through a combination of both.

Employer contributions may be fixed, discretionary, conditional, matching, or based upon another formula established by the Employer.

Employee contributions may be made through payroll deduction, Section 125 salary reduction, after-tax payroll deduction, direct payment, or another lawful method.

Each Benefit Appendix identifies the funding method applicable to that benefit.

Specific funding for each benefit shall be described in the applicable Benefit Appendix.

General funding allowances are provided by the employer each plan period as part of the enrollment materials. General allowances are considered "flex-credits" or "health flex-credits" using the IRS' terminology. These constitute compensation arrangements where employees receive special compensation subject to some terms under the plan. Again, any special terms are specified in Appendix A. The definitions of these allowances, inasmuch as they effect the tax or ACA compliance treamtent of such compensation, are dictated by those terms.

---

# Plan Administration

The Employer appoints the Plan Administrator identified in Appendix A.

The Plan Administrator is responsible for administering the Plan, including:

- interpreting Plan provisions
- determining eligibility
- maintaining Plan records
- resolving administrative questions
- correcting administrative errors
- delegating responsibilities when appropriate
- adopting reasonable administrative procedures

The Plan Administrator shall exercise discretionary authority only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

---

# Claims and Appeals

Every Participant has the right to request benefits for which they believe they are eligible.

Claims shall be decided fairly and consistently according to this Plan, the applicable Benefit Appendix, and applicable law.

A denial shall explain the reason for the decision and any additional information necessary to perfect the claim.

Participants may appeal denied claims according to the procedures applicable to the benefit involved.

Where ERISA claims procedures apply, they shall be administered in accordance with applicable regulations.

---

# Participant Rights

Participants are entitled to the rights provided by applicable law and the applicable Benefit Appendix.

Depending upon the benefits offered, Participants may have rights relating to:

- claims and appeals
- continuation coverage
- privacy protections
- access to Plan information
- reimbursement requests
- benefit elections

Additional participant rights are described in the applicable Benefit Appendices.

---

# Privacy

The Employer will protect participant information as required by applicable law.

Where HIPAA applies, the Employer shall maintain policies and procedures consistent with HIPAA's privacy and security requirements.

Additional privacy information may be provided in separate notices incorporated into this Plan.

---

# Amendment

The Employer may amend this Plan at any time, subject to applicable law.

Unless otherwise required by law, amendments become effective on the date specified by the Employer.

An amendment may modify this Core Plan Document, a Benefit Appendix, or any incorporated schedule.

The Employer may:

* add new benefits;
* discontinue benefits;
* amend contribution amounts;
* modify eligibility rules;
* replace Benefit Appendices;
* adopt new administrative procedures;
* amend this Core Plan Document.

---

# Termination

The Employer may terminate this Plan or any individual Benefit Appendix at any time, subject to applicable law.

Termination of one Benefit Appendix does not terminate the remainder of the Plan unless expressly stated.

Termination does not affect claims or rights that arose before the effective date of termination except as permitted by law.

If the Plan terminates, no new benefits will accrue after the termination date unless required by law.

Claims arising before termination shall continue to be administered according to the Plan terms in effect when the claim arose.

Terminating one benefit does not terminate the remainder of the Plan.

---

# No Employment Contract

Nothing in this Plan guarantees employment, continued employment, or any particular level of compensation.

Employment remains subject to the Employer's normal employment policies and applicable law.

---

# Non-Discrimination

The Employer intends to administer this Plan in compliance with all applicable nondiscrimination requirements under federal law.

Where nondiscrimination testing applies to a particular benefit, those requirements shall be administered under the applicable Benefit Appendix.

---

# Incorporation of Benefit Appendices

The documents listed in Appendix B are incorporated into and form part of this Plan.

Each incorporated Benefit Appendix has the same legal effect as though fully contained within this document.

The Employer may adopt additional Benefit Appendices or retire existing Benefit Appendices through written amendment.

---

# Execution

This Core Plan Document becomes effective on the Effective Date identified in Appendix A and shall remain in effect until amended or terminated by the Employer.

By adopting this document and its incorporated Benefit Appendices, the Employer establishes the CommonCare Welfare Benefit Plan.
