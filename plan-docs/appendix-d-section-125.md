# Appendix D — Section 125 Elections and Employer Flex Credits

## Establishment and scope

${sponsor.legalName} establishes the Section 125 cafeteria-plan arrangement described in this Appendix as an identifiable part of ${plan.name}. This Appendix is adopted with and forms part of the Core Plan Document. Its Plan Year is ${plan.year.startDate} through ${plan.year.endDate}. The Employer retains the adoption record and the terms effective for that Plan Year.

This arrangement permits eligible Employees to choose between taxable compensation and the qualified benefits offered through the Plan. The Core Plan Document, this Appendix, and applicable benefit provisions together state its written terms. A benefit is available for pre-tax election only to the extent it is offered by the Employer and qualifies for the intended treatment under the Internal Revenue Code. Offering an option in an enrollment system does not itself make the option a qualified benefit.

## Eligibility and available benefits

Participation is limited to Employees eligible under the Plan's eligibility rules and the applicable benefit provisions. An individual who is ineligible for cafeteria-plan participation under the Code, including a self-employed individual or a more-than-two-percent S corporation shareholder, may not participate in this Section 125 arrangement. Coverage of eligible family members through an Employee's election remains subject to the applicable benefit's rules.

The available qualified benefits are those offered through the Plan and identified as eligible for pre-tax election in the enrollment materials. Depending on the benefits offered, these may include eligible health coverage premiums, Health FSA benefits, dependent care assistance, or HSA contributions. Each benefit retains its own eligibility, coverage period, contribution limits, substantiation requirements, and governing terms. Exchange-qualified health plan premiums are not eligible for Section 125 salary reduction. An HRA remains funded by the Employer and is not funded through Employee salary reduction.

## Employer allowances and flex credits

The Employer may provide employer-funded allowances or flex credits in addition to, or instead of, Employee salary reductions. The Employer determines the available amounts and permitted uses under its contribution settings. These amounts may vary by eligible employee class and other applicable participant or coverage circumstances, subject to the applicable nondiscrimination and benefit-specific rules. This Appendix does not require the Employer to reproduce every class formula or every participant-specific calculation in the global Plan Document.

Before an Employee makes the applicable election, the enrollment workflow communicates that Employee's available Employer contribution, the benefits toward which it may be applied, any conditions on allocation or cash availability, and the resulting Employee contribution. The Employer retains the contribution settings and employee-specific disclosures applicable to the election, as described in Appendix C. A later configuration change does not rewrite the terms disclosed for an earlier coverage period or authorize a retroactive election change.

An amount may be elected as taxable cash only when the Employer's communicated terms expressly make that amount cashable and any conditions on that election are satisfied. Cash payments are treated as taxable compensation. A restricted credit may be used only for its stated purposes; displaying an allowance or unused balance does not create a right to cash. Restricted HRA or FSA benefits are not converted into cashable compensation. No credit may be allocated or reimbursed twice for the same expense.

## Employee salary reductions and contribution limits

An eligible Employee may authorize prospective salary reductions to pay the Employee's share of eligible qualified benefits. The election identifies the benefits, coverage period, and payroll reductions. The Employer applies available Employer credits according to their permitted uses and the Employee's valid elections; the Employee may pay an eligible remaining amount through salary reduction, subject to this Appendix and the applicable benefit terms.

The maximum salary reduction is the amount elected for the eligible benefits offered for the coverage period, limited to the Employee's available compensation for that period and any lower benefit-specific or Employer-adopted limit. Salary reduction may not exceed the amount required to fund the elected benefit after applicable Employer contributions. For a Health FSA, the maximum annual Employee salary reduction is the indexed limit under Code Section 125(i), or a lower Employer-adopted limit communicated before election. Amounts otherwise available as taxable compensation that are elected into a Health FSA are classified and counted toward the salary-reduction limit as required by the Code; calling them Employer credits does not change their treatment. Other benefits remain subject to their applicable Code limits and adopted benefit terms. A Health FSA salary-reduction limit is not a general ceiling on all Employer allowances and is not automatically the limit for an HSA, dependent care arrangement, or premium payment.

## Election procedure and permitted changes

Initial elections must be made through the enrollment workflow by the communicated deadline and before the compensation or benefit to which they relate becomes currently available, except where applicable law expressly permits otherwise. Annual elections apply to the stated Plan Year or coverage period. New eligibility and permitted midyear changes apply for their applicable coverage periods. The Employee authorizes payroll deductions through the recorded election. Any default or continuing election must be communicated in advance and administered only where permitted by law and the applicable benefit terms.

Elections are irrevocable for their coverage period except for changes this Plan permits under Treasury Regulation Section 1.125-4 or other applicable law. The Plan permits changes for the events recognized by that regulation, subject to its benefit-specific restrictions and consistency requirements. A request must be submitted through the enrollment workflow, with required supporting information, within the communicated period applicable to the event. Approved changes are prospective unless applicable law expressly provides otherwise. Protected-leave elections, contribution payments, and reinstatement follow the Core Plan Document's FMLA and USERRA provisions, including lawful prepayment and catch-up arrangements. HSA contribution elections follow their applicable special election-change rules. An enrollment-system setting cannot authorize a change the governing rules prohibit.

## FSA terms, administration, and records

Where a Health FSA is offered, its adopted benefit provisions must provide the required uniform coverage and use-or-lose rules, including any expressly adopted lawful carryover or grace period. This Appendix does not create a carryover, grace period, cash-out, or additional reimbursement right. Dependent care assistance and other qualified benefits follow their own adopted provisions and applicable rules. Expenses requiring substantiation must be substantiated under the applicable benefit's requirements before reimbursement or other approval as required by law.

The Plan Administrator administers these terms consistently, retains contribution disclosures and elections, applies payroll and tax treatment, and performs applicable nondiscrimination testing. The Employer may amend the arrangement through the Plan's adoption process, subject to applicable timing and amendment rules. The Plan Administrator may make lawful corrections or adjustments necessary to administer the arrangement and satisfy applicable limits and nondiscrimination requirements. These provisions do not authorize deferred compensation or retroactive tax-favored elections where prohibited.
