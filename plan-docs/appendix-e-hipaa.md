# Appendix E — HIPAA Plan Administration

This Appendix forms part of the Core Plan Document. It applies to benefits and information subject to HIPAA and does not make a benefit subject to HIPAA that is otherwise exempt. Protected health information (**PHI**) and electronic PHI have the meanings given in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164. References to the Plan Sponsor include a Participating Employer to the extent it receives PHI to administer the Plan.

## Permitted disclosures and sponsor certification

The Plan may disclose PHI to the Plan Sponsor only as HIPAA permits. For Plan administration, the Plan Sponsor may use or disclose PHI only to perform eligibility and enrollment administration, claims and appeals administration, payment, coordination of benefits, auditing, and other lawful Plan-administration functions it performs for the Plan, subject to the minimum-necessary requirements where applicable. These permissions do not authorize employment uses or administration of another employer benefit plan.

Before disclosing PHI for Plan administration under 45 CFR Section 164.504(f)(2), the Plan must receive the Plan Sponsor's certification that the governing documents incorporate the required provisions and that the Plan Sponsor agrees to comply with them. Adoption of this Appendix does not replace that certification. The Plan Administrator retains the certification. The Plan will not permit an insurer, HMO, or other administrator to disclose PHI to the Plan Sponsor contrary to these restrictions and will include the required sponsor-disclosure statement in the applicable notice of privacy practices.

HIPAA's separate permissions for enrollment/disenrollment information, summary health information used to obtain premium bids or modify, amend, or terminate the Plan, and disclosures under a valid individual authorization remain subject to their applicable conditions. They do not authorize unrestricted access to claims or medical information.

## Plan Sponsor commitments

For PHI received from the Plan for Plan administration, the Plan Sponsor agrees to:

- Use or disclose PHI only as permitted or required by these governing documents or as required by law.
- Ensure that agents receiving PHI agree to the same restrictions and conditions applicable to the Plan Sponsor.
- Not use or disclose PHI for employment-related actions or decisions or in connection with another benefit or employee benefit plan of the Plan Sponsor.
- Report to the Plan any use or disclosure inconsistent with these provisions of which it becomes aware.
- Make PHI available for individual access under 45 CFR Section 164.524, make PHI available for amendment and incorporate amendments under Section 164.526, and make information available for an accounting of disclosures under Section 164.528.
- Make internal practices, books, and records concerning use and disclosure of PHI received from the Plan available to the Secretary of Health and Human Services to determine the Plan's compliance.
- When PHI is no longer needed for the purpose of disclosure, return or destroy it if feasible and retain no copies. If return or destruction is infeasible, limit further uses and disclosures to the purposes making return or destruction infeasible.
- Maintain the separation, access restrictions, and noncompliance mechanism below.

Any separately authorized disclosure must satisfy HIPAA independently; Plan-administration access does not itself authorize an employment-related use.

## Separation and authorized personnel

Only the following persons or classes of persons under the Plan Sponsor's control may access PHI received for Plan administration: the Plan Administrator and its staff assigned to Plan administration; benefits and human-resources personnel assigned to Plan eligibility, enrollment, claims, appeals, or participant assistance; payroll and finance personnel assigned to Plan contributions, payments, reconciliation, or audits; members of a committee assigned Plan claims, appeals, or fiduciary administration; and privacy, security, legal, and compliance personnel assigned to support these Plan functions. Each person's access is restricted to the PHI needed for the assigned Plan-administration function. Membership in a department alone does not authorize access, and a person acting in an employment-management role may not use Plan PHI for that role.

The Plan Administrator will maintain individual access authorizations, review access, and terminate access when the assigned function ends. Adding a class outside those described above requires a governing-document amendment before access is granted.

Suspected noncompliance must be reported to the Plan Administrator or designated privacy official. The Plan Sponsor will investigate, document findings, stop unauthorized access or disclosure, mitigate harm where practicable, apply appropriate disciplinary or contractual sanctions, and implement corrective measures. Required breach notifications and other reports remain subject to HIPAA and applicable law. Retaliation for exercising privacy rights or reporting concerns is prohibited.

## Electronic PHI safeguards

Where 45 CFR Section 164.314(b) applies, the Plan Sponsor will reasonably and appropriately safeguard electronic PHI it creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf of the Plan. It will implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards protecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability; support the required separation through reasonable and appropriate security measures; ensure agents receiving electronic PHI agree to implement reasonable and appropriate security measures; and report to the Plan any security incident of which it becomes aware.

## Privacy and security administration

The Plan Administrator is responsible for arranging HIPAA compliance and serves as the privacy official, privacy complaint contact, and security official unless it designates another person in the Plan's retained administrative records. A designation assigns responsibility without expanding access under this Appendix. The Plan will maintain required policies, training, safeguards, complaint records, notices of privacy practices, and business associate agreements. Participants may contact ${administration.planAdministrator.contact.formatted} for the current privacy contact, notice, or assistance exercising applicable privacy rights. This Appendix is a governing provision and does not replace the notice of privacy practices or a required business associate agreement.
