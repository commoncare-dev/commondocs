## CommonFunds — Optional Dependent Care Assistance

This summary applies only when the Employer expressly adopts the DCAP rider. It describes a separate Section 129 program, commonly called a dependent-care FSA. It is not the CommonFunds Health FSA or HRA. Under the rider's reimbursement-only design it is intended not to be an ERISA benefit; the surrounding SPD's ERISA rights apply only to ERISA-covered benefits.

| Feature | Your program |
|---|---|
| Effective date | ${plan.year.startDate|dateLong} |
| Adopted total annual Plan limit | The limit stated in the adopted DCAP terms and enrollment records, subject to lower applicable limits |
| Availability | Credited contributions actually available, less prior reimbursements; no Health FSA uniform coverage |
| Eligible care | Work-related care satisfying Sections 21 and 129 for an eligible child under 13 or qualifying person incapable of self-care |
| Claim filing | Within the filing period stated in the adopted DCAP terms after the earlier of Plan Year end or end of participation, for care provided during participation |
| Carryover, grace period, and post-termination spend-down | None adopted |
| Claims / review | ${administration.serviceContacts.claims.formatted} / ${administration.serviceContacts.appeals.formatted} |

For services provided in taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025, the federal exclusion is $7,500, or $3,750 if married filing separately, unless the law changes. For a joint return, $7,500 is the combined limit for both spouses across all employers and programs; for separate returns, each spouse's exclusion is limited to $3,750 under the applicable marital-status rules. Employer and Employee funding count together. Earned-income and other eligibility limits may reduce the exclusion. State tax treatment may differ. Report other DCAP benefits and relevant household changes to the administrator. Complete Form 2441 as required. The same expense cannot be reimbursed twice or used for an incompatible tax credit.

Make prospective elections through enrollment under Appendix D. Only permitted consistent election changes are allowed. Medical-only credits and unused Health FSA/HRA balances cannot fund DCAP. Payments cannot exceed the available DCAP balance, even when you elected a larger annual amount. Approved amounts may await later contributions within the permitted period; the administrator will distinguish pending payment from a denial. You may request review of a disputed determination under the procedure below.

Provide independent proof of the care, service dates, charges, provider identification, qualifying person's eligibility, and work-related purpose before reimbursement. Advance payment is not enough: care must have been provided. Day care, preschool, before/after-school care, day camp, and eligible adult care may qualify. Kindergarten tuition and higher education, overnight camp, medical care, care after participation ends, and payments to disqualified related persons are excluded. Review the rider for residence, dependency, provider, and spouse-income rules. Unused amounts are forfeited after the claim deadline; there is no cash-out or transfer to medical benefits.

Claims normally receive a written decision within 30 days, with one explained extension of up to 30 days. If denied, request review within 60 days after receiving the denial; a written review decision is due within 60 days. The denial explains reasons, controlling terms, and any missing proof. These are the rider's contractual procedures. The Employer provides annual dependent-care reporting and performs applicable nondiscrimination testing.
