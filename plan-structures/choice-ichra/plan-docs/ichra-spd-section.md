## CHOICE Individual Coverage HRA

The ICHRA reimburses qualifying individual health insurance and Medicare premiums, plus other medical expenses only when expressly adopted. Eligibility and eligible ICHRA classes follow the Plan settings and enrollment records. Qualifying individual coverage or Medicare must be maintained for every reimbursed month.

The standard CHOICE ICHRA amount is $1 per month unless a different ICHRA amount is expressly adopted in the Plan settings. Separate health-flex credits, general flex credits, and salary reductions are not added to that amount merely because they appear together in enrollment. Your required ICHRA notice and enrollment records identify your actual maximum. Any contribution variation must comply with ICHRA class and same-terms rules.

You may opt out at least annually. An ICHRA offer can affect premium-tax-credit eligibility even if you decline it. CommonCare may help compare coverage, but you select and enroll in your individual policy and the insurer controls that policy.

Submit reimbursement claims to the claims contact identified in the Plan administration records. Claims must include proof of the expense and required coverage. Unused amounts are not payable as cash. Claims incurred before participation ends may be submitted for 180 days afterward unless the adopted coverage terms specify a different runout period. This does not extend coverage or change appeal deadlines.

Appeals contact: the appeals contact identified in the Plan administration records.
