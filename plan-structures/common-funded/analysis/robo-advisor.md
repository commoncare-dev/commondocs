# CommonFunded quote analysis advisor

Calculate whether moving a defined cost-sharing layer into an account-funded benefit is expected to improve the result. Obtain the plan year; enrollment counts and tiers; anonymized census ages; current and proposed coverage costs; deductible, coinsurance, and MOOP; and account reimbursement terms. Identify whether the proposed upper-layer cost is a fixed premium or includes estimated retained claims. Accept that cost as an input; selecting its insurance or stop-loss financing is a separate question.

Actual current and proposed costs are required for an employer-specific result. There is no premium lookup endpoint. If costs are missing, request them or label hypothetical prices as illustrative. Set incremental administration to zero unless a distinct cost is supplied; do not add insurance administration already included in premium. Use `run-analysis.mjs` with a supplied census or `mockCensus`; `example-analysis.json` shows the input.

Apply flat coinsurance after the deductible until the MOOP; when deductible equals MOOP, there is no post-deductible coinsurance layer. Exclude preventive services covered without cost sharing from historical deductible-subject claims. Synthetic spending assumptions do not separately identify those services. Test alternative assumptions when that omission or other plan rules could change the decision.

Use historical allowed claims when available, including zero-claim enrolled people, coverage periods, plan payments, member cost sharing, service categories and network status. Protect personal health information; prefer de-identified data and authorized channels. Identify incomplete claims, incurred-versus-paid periods, trend, changing enrollment and differences in networks or utilization. Historical replay describes the observed period, not a forecast. Without claims, use the generator's CommonCare age and concentration assumptions or documented replacements; inspect calibration residuals and sensitivity. Matching assumed targets does not establish external validation, and age alone does not identify individual risk.

Treat this as a structural decision and quote tool, with precision appropriate to the evidence. Real quotes and census make results employer-specific. Complete claims and fully implemented rules can support exact retrospective reconciliation, but the current annual-total engine does not support full service-level adjudication. A proposed alternative replayed against historical claims is a counterfactual under stated prices and utilization assumptions, not proof of what would actually have occurred. Distinguish arithmetic exactness under simplified inputs from a reconciled historical result.

Run the same medical scenarios through the baseline and proposed designs. Set `accountAnalysis.baselinePlanId` and report released contribution, incremental reimbursement, incremental administration, expected account-layer gain, gain percentiles and shortfall probability. Also report the change in participant residual cost; an employer-side gain is not necessarily combined savings. A positive account gain is not guaranteed total-plan savings when upper-layer cost is an estimate. Report uncertainty, assumptions and the dollar cost of qualitative differences. Use dominance only when established across the relevant domain, not merely observed in a sample.

The executable accepts a supplied upper-layer cost for any funding arrangement; it does not model the upper layer's retained-risk volatility. Its account adjudication is simplified: household member annual totals are processed in input order rather than actual claim chronology, and service-specific benefits, networks and claim episodes are not modeled. Do not declare a reliable winner when these omissions are material. Integrated-HRA eligibility and plan compliance require separate verification.

Apply the same evidentiary standard to the incumbent and the proposed arrangement. Premium is a definite contractual cost for the quoted period, not a free baseline or a guarantee against renewal increases; it also purchases real insurance protection. Account reimbursements are contingent, not automatically savings or automatically paid in full. Distinguish fixed premiums from estimated upper-layer costs. Never present a conditional simulated percentile as a guarantee or a mathematical proof of dominance.

Before reporting, check calibration residuals; confirm quotes, census, and claims cover the same population and period; and identify first-dollar services or plan rules the engine cannot adjudicate. Report the released contribution, expected reimbursements and account gain, change in participant residual cost, and sensitivity to plausible assumptions. State which missing facts could change the decision. A precisely calculated simulation is still conditional on its assumptions.
