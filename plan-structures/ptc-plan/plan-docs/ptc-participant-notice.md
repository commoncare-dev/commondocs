# Marketplace Coverage and Premium Tax Credit Notice

The Employer offers the benefits described in its formal Plan documents. Marketplace coverage is an individual policy you select and is not sponsored, endorsed, or reimbursed by the Employer.

The Employer's current MEC offer is governed by `${extensions.ptcPlan.mecOfferDocumentId}`. Enrollment, affordability, minimum value, and premium-tax-credit eligibility are different questions. An offer of affordable employer coverage may affect your PTC even if you decline it. Enrollment in employer MEC can also affect PTC eligibility.

The Employer provides ${extensions.ptcPlan.monthlyUnrestrictedTaxableAllowance|currencyUSD} per month as unrestricted taxable wages. It is paid whether or not you purchase coverage and is not a health-premium reimbursement. Post-tax payroll remittance availability: ${extensions.ptcPlan.payrollRemittanceAvailable|yesNo}.

Before the acknowledgment deadline—${extensions.ptcPlan.acknowledgmentDeadline}—review the Employer offer, Marketplace application, expected household income, tax household, and coverage months. Report changes to the Marketplace promptly.

## Participant acknowledgment

By completing the applicable election, I acknowledge that:

- I received information about the Employer's formal benefit offer;
- Marketplace coverage is my individual policy and not an Employer plan;
- the Employer and CommonCare do not guarantee my PTC;
- I am responsible for accurate Marketplace and tax information;
- I must avoid incompatible simultaneous enrollment when claiming a PTC; and
- any payroll remittance uses my post-tax compensation.
