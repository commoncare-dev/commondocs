# DPC Companion Coverage Appendix

Version: ${extensions.dpcCompanion.document.version}

Effective Date: ${extensions.dpcCompanion.document.effectiveDate|dateLong}

## 1. Adoption and coverage election

The Employer adopts DPC Companion as an independently selectable direct primary care (DPC) option under the Plan. Eligible Participants may select the offered DPC coverage alongside their other benefit elections. Available providers, covered services, eligible persons, fees, and coverage periods are established through the applicable coverage terms and disclosed through enrollment.

DPC supplies the care described in the selected coverage. It does not itself provide major medical insurance or establish eligibility for any other benefit. The Core Plan Document supplies the common Plan terms.

## 2. Allowance and employee contributions

The Employer may make an allowance available toward DPC through the Plan's adopted benefits. Participants may also elect their own contributions through an available Health FSA, subject to the applicable cafeteria-plan election and contribution rules. Amounts and elections are determined through the Plan's contribution settings and enrollment records as described in Appendix C; this Appendix does not establish a second allowance or separate account.

A common allowance or DPC election may be funded differently for different Participants. The funding rights, governing benefit terms, and Participant's other coverage and elections determine the legal classification. Employer credits available as taxable cash and elected into the Health FSA are salary reduction. Genuinely Employer-only HRA amounts remain HRA funding. An amount already committed to one benefit does not become available under another merely because the Participant cannot use the first benefit.

## 3. Applicable qualified benefits

Eligible DPC medical expenses may be paid through the following benefits only to the extent adopted and available to the affected Participant:

- **Health FSA.** Employer contributions and Participant salary reductions retain their respective classifications. The Health FSA's eligible-expense scope, excepted-benefit conditions, annual limits, deductible restrictions, and uniform-coverage rule apply.
- **Integrated group-plan HRA.** Payment requires the qualifying group coverage and other conditions of the adopted integration method. The benefit is not an EBHRA and does not use the EBHRA limit.
- **Excepted-benefit HRA.** Payment requires the applicable supporting group coverage offer and compliance with the EBHRA's annual allocation limit and other excepted-benefit conditions. This component is not offered to the same Participant as an ICHRA.
- **ICHRA.** Payment requires ICHRA-qualifying individual coverage or Medicare and an adopted expense scope permitting the DPC medical expense. Available funds may pay eligible nonpremium care without first reimbursing an insurance premium. A spouse's employer group coverage alone does not satisfy ICHRA's individual-coverage requirement.
- **HSA.** Where available, employee-owned HSA funds may pay eligible DPC expenses under section 223. Contribution eligibility and expense eligibility are separate requirements; this Appendix does not convert an HRA or FSA allowance into HSA assets.

Where CommonFunds is adopted, its allocation and calculation provisions govern the applicable FSA and HRA portions. The same DPC expense may be paid from more than one eligible portion, but no dollar of expense may be reimbursed twice. Underlying benefit documents govern entitlement, available amounts, claims, and unused balances. DPC Companion does not enlarge those rights.

## 4. One enrollment and payment experience

CommonCare administers DPC through its existing enrollment and provider-payment structure. The Participant's DPC selection can remain the same whether the expense is funded through a Health FSA, HRA, ICHRA, or eligible combination. Provider agreements and employee payment elections follow that existing structure.

CommonCare may group employee payments to a provider while preserving the legal source of each Participant's payment. Grouped payment does not pool Participants' benefit rights, change the character of their contributions, or authorize payment of an expense excluded by the underlying benefit.

Only eligible, properly substantiated medical expenses may receive qualified-benefit treatment. The underlying component governs when an expense is incurred, any deductible, and reimbursement availability. Employee responsibility for amounts not covered by an available benefit follows the coverage terms and enrollment election. Membership cancellation does not independently permit a midyear cafeteria-plan election change.

## 5. Relationship to the governing Plan

This Appendix establishes DPC as a coverage election and coordinates the benefits that may pay for it. It does not independently establish an HRA, Health FSA, or HSA. The adopted underlying benefit governs its contribution classification, eligibility, expense scope, claims, continuation, and year-end treatment. The Core Plan Document governs common administration and Participant rights.
