## DPC Companion

DPC Companion lets you select direct primary care as an independent option alongside your other benefits. Your enrollment materials describe the available providers, covered services, eligible household members, price, and coverage period. DPC supplies the care described in that option; it does not replace major medical coverage.

### Your allowance and contributions

Your Employer may make an allowance available toward DPC. You may also elect your own pre-tax contributions through an available Health FSA, within its election and contribution rules. Your amounts and elections are disclosed through enrollment.

You can select the same DPC option through the same enrollment experience even when the benefit paying for it differs. Depending on your other coverage, elections, and the adopted Plan terms, payment may come from an eligible Health FSA, integrated HRA, excepted-benefit HRA, ICHRA, or a combination. Eligible HSA funds are another possible source.

The common allowance does not make every source available to everyone. For example, an ICHRA requires qualifying individual coverage or Medicare and must permit the DPC expense; a spouse's employer group coverage alone is insufficient. An employee's additional Health FSA election may supply funds when another source cannot pay, subject to the FSA's separate requirements. An unavailable ICHRA amount does not automatically become FSA money.

### One DPC payment experience

CommonCare handles your election and provider payment through its existing enrollment and provider-payment structure. It can group payments to the provider while preserving the source of each employee's funds. You do not receive a different DPC membership merely because the underlying benefit classification differs.

The benefit supplying the funds determines eligibility, available amounts, any deductible, and applicable limits. Health FSA availability follows its uniform-coverage rule; HRA availability follows the HRA's terms. The same expense cannot be reimbursed twice, including from an HSA. You remain responsible for amounts assigned to you under your coverage terms and election that no available benefit pays.

### Governing terms and help

The DPC Companion Coverage Appendix and your underlying benefit documents determine your benefits. Their claims, continuation, and year-end rules apply. Canceling DPC does not itself permit a midyear pre-tax election change.
