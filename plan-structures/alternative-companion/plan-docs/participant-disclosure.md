# Alternative Companion Participant Disclosure

The products available through Alternative Companion are **not employer sponsored, are not part of the Employer's group health plan, and are voluntary individual purchases paid with post-tax funds**.

These products may include health care sharing, crowdfunding or member-support arrangements, and related cash-pay strategies. They may not be insurance, may not guarantee payment, may limit pre-existing conditions, may apply membership or lifestyle standards, and may leave you responsible for expenses that major medical insurance would cover.

The Employer does not recommend, select, negotiate, fund, or administer these products. Product questions, membership decisions, payments, and disputes remain between you and the provider. ${extensions.alternativeCompanion.marketplaceOperator} provides the comparison environment and can be contacted at ${extensions.alternativeCompanion.marketplaceContact}.

Post-tax payroll remittance availability: ${extensions.alternativeCompanion.postTaxPayrollAvailable|yesNo}. The Employer may also pay ${extensions.alternativeCompanion.monthlyUnrestrictedTaxableAllowance|currencyUSD} per month as unrestricted taxable wages. Those wages are paid whether or not you purchase an alternative.

Before ${extensions.alternativeCompanion.acknowledgmentDeadline}, review the product agreement, eligibility, exclusions, payment or sharing rules, maximum or uncapped exposure, provider access, treatment of pre-existing conditions, and the consequences of leaving or declining regulated insurance.

By electing an independent option, I acknowledge the boundaries described above and understand that the election does not enroll me in an Employer-sponsored benefit.
